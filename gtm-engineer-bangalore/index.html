How engagements work, and what about budget?

Budget is never my first filter. If the problem is interesting and you care about doing it properly, we'll find a scope that fits, often a smaller first build that pays for itself before we expand. I trust the quality of what I ship, and a happy client matters more to me than a bigger first invoice. That's why a lot of my work comes from founders who reached out with a lean budget.

  • Build sprint: one system end to end, fixed scope and fixed price, handed over with docs.
  • Fractional GTM engineer: ongoing list building, campaigns, infrastructure and reporting on a monthly retainer.
  • GTM audit: a teardown of your data, sending infrastructure, copy and CRM, with a prioritised fix list.

You own everything that gets built: the tables, workflows, code and documentation stay in your accounts.

FAQ: hiring a GTM engineer in Bangalore

Is it Bangalore or Bengaluru?

Both refer to the same city. It was officially renamed Bengaluru in 2014, and Bangalore is still widely used. Either way, that's where I'm based.

Do you work US or UK hours from Bangalore?

I overlap with UK and European business hours in my afternoon and with US mornings in my evening. Calls are scheduled in your time zone, and build work happens during your night so progress is waiting when you start your day.

Do you meet clients in person in Bengaluru?

Work is remote by default. For teams in Bengaluru, an in-person working session is easy to arrange when it helps, for example at kickoff or handover.

Can you work alongside our SDRs or RevOps team?

Yes. Most engagements plug into an existing team: I build the data, infrastructure and automation layer, document it, and train the team to run it. Everything built stays in your own accounts.

What size of company do you work with?

Mostly B2B SaaS from seed to growth stage, across AI, fintech, adtech, marketplaces and recruiting, plus agencies that run outbound for clients. Budget is not the first filter: interesting problems with lean budgets are welcome.

How much does a GTM engineer in Bangalore cost?

Pricing depends on scope rather than an hourly rate card. Build sprints are fixed price, fractional GTM engineering is a monthly retainer, and an audit is a small fixed fee. Share your budget on a call and I'll tell you honestly what it can achieve.
