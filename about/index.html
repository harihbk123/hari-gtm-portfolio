The short version

I build the systems that take a lead from first signal to booked meeting with as little manual work as possible. That means signal-based lists, Clay enrichment, cold email infrastructure run at fleet scale, and AI agents that handle the operational work, so a small team can do what used to need a floor of SDRs and researchers.

I work AI-native. Most of what I've shipped in the last year, from a cold email command center managing thousands of inboxes to a 100+ tool MCP server and an AI analyst that lives in Slack, was designed and built with Claude Code. See the case studies.

How I got here

I started in 2016 at Tracxn, the Bengaluru startup intelligence platform, managing a team of 18+ research executives. I rebuilt the SOPs for data collection and validation, introduced objective KRI tracking, and doubled average team productivity within the first year.

At Instawork, the San Francisco gig-economy platform, I led research from Bengaluru for the US business development team: target identification, contact validation and market coverage tracking by geography and vertical. A stint running data intelligence for a stealth engagement followed, then lead generation for fintech at Scienaptic AI, finding decision-makers at credit unions, community banks and lenders.

At LNF People I rebuilt a recruiting firm's research function from manual spreadsheets into a Clay-driven workflow. That was the turning point: the same work, done by systems, was faster and better. Since then I've been a GTM engineer, at Runway on its fintech go-to-market and at Opflow (Tuuma), engineering the outbound machine for 10+ B2B clients.

What I believe about GTM work

  • Systems over headcount. If a person does it every week, it should probably be a system.
  • Data honesty. Unknown is never zero, and a confident wrong number is worse than no number.
  • Deliverability is the product. The best copy in the world does nothing from a burned domain.
  • Budget is a conversation, not a gate. Interesting problems with lean budgets are welcome. A happy client matters more than a bigger invoice.
  • You own what I build. Tables, workflows, code and docs stay in your accounts.
